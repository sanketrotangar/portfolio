How it works under the hood (for the skeptical reader)

Front desk receives voice calls or text messages via standard webhooks. Incoming queries are checked against your private documents using semantic search and deterministic rule checks. The system formats answers in structured JSON to guarantee numbers, policies, and links are never hallucinated. Memory writes records directly to your secure database or spreadsheet. If a query falls outside approved documents, the system triggers a human handoff instantly.

Transparency

Questions people actually ask.

Candid information about privacy, accuracy, and operational handoffs.

Where does my data go? +

Your customer data and company files stay in systems and databases you control. We configure the connections in your accounts. Your proprietary documents and customer contact lists are never used to train public machine learning models.

What if it gets an answer wrong? +

The system is strictly instructed to say "I do not have that confirmed in our documents, let me notify our staff" whenever a fact is not explicitly present in your verified files. It does not guess prices, discounts, or terms. If uncertainty is detected, it alerts your team immediately.

How are handoffs to a human handled? +

If a customer requests a person, expresses frustration, or has a custom requirement outside standard rules, the conversation is flagged. Your staff receives an immediate notification via WhatsApp, SMS, or email with the chat summary so anyone can step in directly.

Who owns it at the end? +

You own all code, configurations, database records, and document indexes. There is no vendor lock-in or closed proprietary platform. If you ever choose to manage it yourself or have another engineer maintain it, you retain full access to everything.

What does it cost? +

The initial 30-minute diagnostic check is free. If we proceed to a 14-day pilot, it is a single fixed fee quoted upfront based on your channels and complexity. Ongoing support is a predictable flat monthly retainer without hidden per-message markups.

Does it work in my language? +

Yes. The system works in the languages your customers use. It automatically detects the language of incoming messages or voice calls and responds accurately in that same language.

What can't it do? +

It cannot replace human judgment on complex disputes or delicate negotiations. It cannot fix broken business operations or faulty inventory records. It will not blast unsolicited spam messages. It is built to answer repetitive questions, capture accurate data, and execute scheduled tasks reliably.
