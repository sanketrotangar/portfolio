About

I build AI systems that run in production.

I'm a backend engineer from Goa who builds the AI layer and the systems around it: retrieval, voice, agents, and the APIs that keep them reliable. I studied Electronics and Computer Engineering at PCCE Goa (CGPA 9.23) and have done three engineering internships.

I like problems with real constraints: who may see which document, how retrieval stays accurate, what a voice agent does when a call goes wrong. My projects are built around those questions, not just a model call.

With an electronics background, I build the software side of hardware systems: the backend, cloud and dashboards behind IoT networks like AeroConnect, which ran on five solar-powered nodes in Margao, Goa. Off the clock, I'm usually planning a road trip.
