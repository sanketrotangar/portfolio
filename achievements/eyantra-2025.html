The project

AeroConnect is a solar-powered air-quality monitoring network. Sensor nodes report readings over a mesh link, and the system stores them for forecasting and for a live dashboard.

The build ran on five solar-powered nodes in Margao, Goa. It started as a competition entry and went through a year of review rounds to reach the finals at IIT Bombay, finishing among the top 12 teams in India.

Built as a team of 3. I worked on the backend, the cloud side and the dashboard behind the nodes.
