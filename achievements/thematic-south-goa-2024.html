The project

Third Eye was built for a state-level project competition on a set theme, and was judged among the top entries in South Goa. The result carried it into the national round and onto the Goa delegation at the National Youth Festival in January 2025.

Built as a team of 3, with the engineering split across the hardware and the software that collects and reads the data.
