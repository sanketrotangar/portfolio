The project

EcoNode+ is a smart waste management system built around the idea that collection routes should follow what a bin actually holds, not a fixed schedule. Sensor readings report fill level, and the system uses them to plan pickups and surface bins that need attention.

The challenge was judged across problem relevance, feasibility and the quality of the working prototype. EcoNode+ won the Student Innovators category and was selected for co-creation with the Government of Goa, which is the next step for the build.

Built as a team of 3. My work covered the backend, the sensor data pipeline and the dashboard the operators use.
