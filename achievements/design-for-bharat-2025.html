The project

Forest areas rarely have reliable mobile coverage, so a monitoring system cannot assume the internet is there. WildGuard is built as an offline-first network: nodes communicate with each other without a cellular link and raise an alert when activity looks like poaching or illegal hunting.

The build was presented at the IIT Goa exhibition, where it was judged the state winner and placed among the top three Goa teams in the challenge.

Built as a team of 3. My focus was the communication layer and the backend that receives and classifies incident reports.
