The project

Night Vigil connects three questions that a check-in sheet cannot answer: is the officer the person assigned to the duty, are they inside the assigned boundary, and is patrol movement actually being recorded during the shift.

The build pairs a React Native app with a Flask backend and MySQL. Officers authenticate with an OTP, complete a selfie identity check, check in inside the geofence, and the app keeps recording location in the background until check-out. The server evaluates distance from the geofence and keeps the live position separate from the historical patrol trail.

Built as a team of 4 across a 48-hour window, and judged first place. The full engineering write-up lives on the project page.
